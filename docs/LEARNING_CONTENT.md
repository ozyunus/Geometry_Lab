# Learning content and pedagogy

## Content architecture
Skill paths rather than school grades: (1) Shapes and lines, (2) Circles and constructions, (3) Symmetry and transformations, (4) Polygons and stars, (5) Tessellations and repetition, (6) Decode world patterns, (7) Original design.

Each lesson has: learning goal; 1-sentence invitation; interactive task; geometry-based validation; Notice prompt; Why It Works (plain-language); Math Details (optional); Try Variation; Free Create transfer. Text in both languages. Support hints and retry without punitive scoring.

## Initial rosette path: 8 lessons
1. What is a circle? / Çember nedir? — choose center, adjust radius.
2. Compass and radius / Pergel ve yarıçap — same radius, different center.
3. Overlapping circles / Kesişen çemberler — create two equal circles whose centers are one radius apart.
4. Six-petal rosette / Altı yapraklı çiçek — six equal circles with centers at 60° intervals around the initial center; understand construction.
5. Pattern variations / Desen çeşitleri — stroke, color, hidden construction lines.
6. Rotation / Döndürme — rotate repeated primitives and compare.
7. Eight-point star / Sekiz köşeli yıldız — rotate a second square 45° around a shared center; understand silhouette vs polygon intersections.
8. Remix / Kendi yorumun — create, save, export.

## Three-layer explanation system
- Do It / Uygula: instructions, minimal terminology.
- Why It Works / Neden Böyle?: one short causal explanation grounded in the actual geometry.
- Math Details / Matematik Detayı: optional angle, radius, coordinates, transformation and (only where meaningful) trigonometry.

## Examples, mathematically sound
- Six equally spaced positions on a full turn: 360° / 6 = 60°. For equal-radius circles with surrounding centers on a radius-r circle, adjacent centers are distance r apart.
- Square rotational symmetry: quarter-turn = 90°. Rotating a second congruent centered square 45° interleaves eight corner directions; two overlapping squares yield an eight-point star *silhouette*, not necessarily a single simple eight-vertex polygon.
- Coordinate rotation (advanced): x' = x cos θ − y sin θ; y' = x sin θ + y cos θ (about origin; translate to/from a chosen center).
- Distinguish circle boundary (çember) from filled disk (daire) in Turkish UI.

## Culture and global perspective
Explore world traditions without claiming universal origins. Use named cultural contexts only with reliable attribution. Sample: Anatolian Seljuk star geometry; Ottoman architectural tile geometry; Persian girih; Moroccan zellige; Andalusian motifs; Japanese kumiko; modern tessellations. Islamic geometric art gets a substantial dedicated section, but the platform is not Turkey-only. Distinguish geometric construction demonstration from a faithful reconstruction of a specific historic artifact. For unverified imagery label "Inspired by / Esinlenilmiştir". Never invent museum object dates or provenance.

## Decode activity
Show an illustrated eight-point star; learner selects perceived primitives (squares, lines, angles), toggles two square outlines and 45° rotation, then reconstructs. Give constructive feedback and allow exploring alternative decompositions.
